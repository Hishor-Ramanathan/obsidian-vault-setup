Lists all Plugins which are used and the Purpose of each.

- **Book Search**  
    Automates the creation and organization of book notes by searching for book metadata and generating notes with templates, including cover images and other details.​

- **Copilot**  
    Integrates AI features into Obsidian, allowing you to chat with your notes, generate summaries, use web and YouTube search, and get agentic support for your projects—similar to having an AI assistant within your vault.​

- **Dataview**  
    Treats your notes like a database, enabling you to create queries to filter, sort, and visualize notes in tables, lists, or tasks based on their metadata and content.
    
- **Diagrams**  
    Allows users to create and embed diagrams (SVG, draw.io, etc.) directly in notes; perfect for flowcharts, mind maps, and visual explanations.​

- **Excalidraw**  
    Adds a sketching tool for freehand drawings, UI mockups, and visual thinking; drawing files are integrated and stored in your vault, with support for automation and scripts.​

- **File Order**  
    Lets you customize the order of files and folders using number prefixes; supports drag-and-drop reordering, useful for organizing your notes' structure.​

- **Folder notes**  
    Lets you attach notes directly to folders, making it easier to create index or overview notes for topics; supports templates and advanced folder handling.​

- **Kanban**  
    Converts notes into Kanban boards to visually manage projects, workflows, and tasks using columns and cards—helpful for content production and idea management.​

- **QuickAdd**  
    Enables fast actions like creating notes from templates, capturing content into specific files, and running macros for complex workflows—all triggered quickly via hotkeys or commands	

- **LanguageTool Integration**
	Advanced grammar and spell checking directly into your notes. It checks your text in real-time for spelling and grammar errors and can even support multiple languages.
